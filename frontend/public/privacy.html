Loadedout Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Loadedout (loadedout.online and the Loadedout Android app) is a personal fitness and lifestyle tracker run by Salman Ranjha. This page explains what data the app handles and why.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, profile picture and, if you sign in with Google, your Google account ID.
  • What you enter: workouts, meals, body weight, schedule, budget, expenses, accounts, inventory and similar entries.
  • Google Calendar (only if you connect it): read-only access to the events on your primary calendar.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to show your data, compute stats, and power the AI features you use (for example meal estimates and workout suggestions). AI features are processed by Google Cloud's Vertex AI, which does not use your data to train its models.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Google user data

When you connect Google Calendar, Loadedout reads your events and copies them into your Loadedout schedule. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in your Google Calendar. Calendar data is used only to show your schedule in the app, is never sold or used for advertising, and is never used to train AI models.

Loadedout's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on servers hosted by Google Cloud in Europe. Access is limited to the app itself and its operator.

Your choices

  • Remove Loadedout's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To get a copy of your data or have your account and data deleted, email us and we will do it within 30 days.
